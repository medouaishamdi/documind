# Voltera V2 electric bike — Owner's manual (fictional product, created for the DocuMind benchmark)

## 1. Safety first

Read this manual before your first ride. Always wear a helmet, check that both brakes work, and make sure the quick-release levers are closed. The Voltera V2 is a pedal-assist bike: the motor only helps while you pedal and stops assisting at 25 km/h, as required by European regulation EN 15194. Do not modify the speed limiter; doing so voids the warranty and may make the bike illegal on public roads.

The maximum total load (rider, luggage and child seat) is 130 kg. The rear rack carries up to 25 kg.

## 2. The battery

The V2 uses a 540 Wh lithium-ion battery integrated into the down tube. In Eco mode a full charge gives a range of about 120 km; in Tour mode about 80 km; in Turbo mode about 45 km. Real range depends on temperature, rider weight, tyre pressure and hills, and it drops by up to 30% below 5 °C.

To remove the battery, insert the key in the lock under the down tube, turn it a quarter turn to the left and pull the battery down gently. The battery weighs 2.9 kg.

## 3. Charging

Use only the supplied 4 A charger. A full charge from empty takes about 4 hours 30 minutes; charging from 0 to 80% takes about 2 hours 45 minutes. Charge the battery at a temperature between 0 °C and 40 °C, indoors, away from flammable materials. The charger LED is red while charging and turns green when the battery is full.

You do not need to wait for the battery to be empty before charging it, and partial charges do not damage it. If the battery is very cold after a winter ride, let it rest at room temperature for one hour before plugging it in.

## 4. Assistance modes and the display

Press the + and - buttons on the handlebar remote to switch between four modes: Off, Eco, Tour and Turbo. Holding the - button for 3 seconds activates the walk-assist mode, which pushes the bike at up to 6 km/h, useful on steep ramps or when carrying luggage.

The display shows speed, battery level, the active mode and the remaining range estimate. Press the power button twice quickly to switch the front and rear lights on or off. The display backlight dims automatically in the dark.

## 5. Error codes

When the system detects a problem, the display shows a code. Most errors clear after switching the bike off and on again.

- E-01: Motor overheating. Stop riding for 15 minutes so the motor can cool down, then continue in a lower assistance mode.
- E-03: Speed sensor signal lost. Check that the spoke magnet on the rear wheel is aligned with the sensor on the frame, within 5 mm.
- E-07: Communication error between the battery and the motor. Remove the battery, clean the contacts with a dry cloth and reinsert it firmly until it clicks.
- E-09: Battery temperature outside the safe range. Move the bike to a place between 0 °C and 40 °C and wait.
- E-12: Brake sensor fault. The motor cuts out when a brake lever is pulled; if one lever stays pressed or its sensor is unplugged, the motor will not assist.
- E-20: Firmware mismatch after an incomplete update. Connect the bike to the Voltera app over Bluetooth and run the update again.

If an error comes back more than three times in a day, contact an authorised Voltera dealer.

## 6. Tyres and brakes

The V2 comes with 27.5 x 2.4 inch puncture-resistant tyres. Recommended pressure is between 2.0 and 2.8 bar; use the lower values on gravel or wet roads for more grip, and the higher values on smooth asphalt for more range. Check the pressure every two weeks.

The hydraulic disc brakes need no cable adjustment. Replace the brake pads when their thickness falls below 1.5 mm, which typically happens every 1,500 to 2,500 km depending on how and where you ride.

## 7. Maintenance schedule

Have the bike serviced by a dealer after the first 500 km, then every 3,000 km or once a year, whichever comes first. The first service checks spoke tension and bolt torque, which settle during the first weeks. Clean and lubricate the chain every 300 km, or after every ride in the rain. Never clean the bike with a pressure washer: water can get into the motor bearings and the electrical connectors.

## 8. Storage and winter

If you do not ride for more than a month, store the battery at between 50% and 70% charge in a dry place between 10 °C and 20 °C, and top it up every three months. Never store a fully empty battery for long periods: it can drop into deep discharge and become unusable, which is not covered by the warranty.

## 9. Transporting the bike

The V2 weighs 24.5 kg with its battery. When carrying it on a car rack, remove the battery and the display first and make sure the rack is rated for electric bikes. Lithium batteries of this size cannot be taken on planes.

## 10. Warranty

The frame is guaranteed for 5 years against manufacturing defects. The motor and electrical components are guaranteed for 2 years. The battery is guaranteed for 2 years or 500 full charge cycles, whichever comes first, and must still hold at least 70% of its original capacity during that period. Wear parts such as tyres, chain, brake pads and cassette are not covered. To make a claim, contact the dealer where you bought the bike with your proof of purchase and the frame number, which is engraved under the bottom bracket.
