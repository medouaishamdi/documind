# Brightwell Labs — Privacy and data protection policy (fictional company, created for the DocuMind benchmark)

## 1. Who we are

Brightwell Labs SAS, based in Lyon, France, is the data controller for the personal data described in this policy. This policy applies to our customers, website visitors, job applicants and suppliers. Questions can be sent to our Data Protection Officer, Claire Moreau, at dpo@brightwell.example or by post to our Lyon office.

## 2. What data we collect

We collect identity and contact data (name, email address, phone number), account data (login, preferences), billing data (invoices and payment status, but never full card numbers, which are handled by our payment provider), usage data from our products (pages viewed, features used, device type) and, for job applicants, the CV and cover letter you send us.

We do not collect sensitive data such as health information or political opinions, and we do not knowingly collect data about children under 15. If we learn that a child under 15 has created an account, we delete it.

## 3. Why we use your data (legal bases)

We use your data to provide the service you subscribed to (performance of a contract), to send invoices and keep accounting records (legal obligation), to improve and secure our products (legitimate interest) and, only if you agree, to send you our newsletter and to use analytics cookies (consent). You can withdraw your consent at any time, for example with the unsubscribe link at the bottom of every newsletter.

## 4. How long we keep it

Customer account data is kept for as long as the account is active and deleted 3 years after the last activity. Invoices are kept for 10 years, as required by French accounting law. Server and security logs are kept for 12 months. CVs of applicants who were not hired are kept for 2 years so that we can contact them about future openings, unless they ask us to delete them earlier. Video surveillance footage from our office entrance is deleted after 30 days. Newsletter subscribers who have not opened any email for 24 months are removed from the list.

## 5. Who we share data with

We never sell personal data. We share it only with subprocessors that help us run the service: our cloud hosting provider, our payment provider, our email delivery service and our customer support tool. Every subprocessor signs a data processing agreement, and we review the list of subprocessors once a year. The current list is available on request from the DPO.

## 6. Transfers outside the European Union

Our main hosting is located in Frankfurt, Germany. A few subprocessors may access data from outside the European Union, for example for 24/7 support. In those cases we rely on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses and additional security measures such as encryption at rest and in transit.

## 7. Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data, to receive it in a portable format, to restrict its use or to object to processing based on legitimate interest. Send your request to dpo@brightwell.example; we may ask for proof of identity. We answer within one month; for complex requests this period can be extended by two more months, in which case we tell you why.

If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can lodge a complaint with the French data protection authority, the CNIL.

## 8. Security

Data is encrypted in transit with TLS 1.2 or higher and at rest with AES-256. Access to production data is restricted to employees who need it for their job, requires multi-factor authentication and is logged. We run an external penetration test every year and a vulnerability scan every week.

## 9. Data breaches

If a personal data breach is likely to create a risk for the people concerned, we notify the CNIL within 72 hours of becoming aware of it. If the risk is high, for example when passwords or financial information may have leaked, we also inform the affected people directly and without undue delay, explaining what happened and what they should do.

## 10. Cookies

Our website uses strictly necessary cookies, which do not require consent, and analytics cookies, which are only set if you accept them in the cookie banner. Your choice is remembered for 6 months, after which the banner is shown again. We do not use advertising cookies.

## 11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when we add a new subprocessor. For significant changes we inform customers by email at least 30 days before they take effect. This version was last updated in September 2026.
