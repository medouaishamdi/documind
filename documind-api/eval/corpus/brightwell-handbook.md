# Brightwell Labs — Employee Handbook (fictional company, created for the DocuMind benchmark)

## 1. Working hours and remote work

Brightwell Labs runs on a 35-hour week. Core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 16:00 Paris time, Monday to Thursday; outside those hours you organise your schedule freely as long as your team agrees. Fridays have no meetings before 14:00 so that everyone gets a block of focused time.

Employees may work remotely up to three days per week after their trial period. Fully remote arrangements are possible for roles approved by the head of department, and require a written agreement reviewed every twelve months. When working from home you must use the company VPN and a private, password-protected Wi-Fi network; public Wi-Fi in cafés or trains is only acceptable through the VPN.

Working abroad is allowed for up to 20 working days per calendar year, in countries where Brightwell has no legal restriction. You must declare the destination to HR at least two weeks before leaving, because some countries create tax obligations for the company after a short stay.

## 2. Paid leave

Full-time employees earn 2.5 days of paid leave per month worked, which adds up to 30 working days per year. In addition, the company grants 10 RTT days per year to compensate for the reduced working week. Leave requests are submitted in the HR portal, Lumen, and must be approved by your manager.

For absences longer than one week, submit the request at least one month in advance. Up to 5 unused days can be carried over to the next year; any remaining days expire on 31 May. Managers are asked to make sure that every team member takes at least two consecutive weeks off between May and October.

## 3. Sick leave and medical appointments

If you are ill, tell your manager before 10:00 on the first day of absence and send the medical certificate within 48 hours. Brightwell tops up the social security sick pay so that you keep 100% of your salary for the first 90 days of sick leave in any rolling twelve-month period.

Medical appointments that cannot be scheduled outside working hours do not need to be taken as leave; simply block the time in your calendar.

## 4. Parental leave

Brightwell offers 16 weeks of fully paid leave to the birth parent and 8 weeks of fully paid leave to the second parent, on top of the statutory minimum. The leave can be split into at most three periods during the first year after the birth or adoption. Parents returning from leave may work at 80% time for full pay during their first month back.

## 5. Expenses and business travel

Business expenses are reimbursed through the expense tool, Ledgerly, within 30 days of the expense. Receipts are mandatory for every amount above 15 euros. Meals during business trips are covered up to 25 euros for lunch and 40 euros for dinner, wine and alcohol excluded.

Train is mandatory for trips under four hours, in second class. Flights must be booked in economy class through the travel agency at least 14 days before departure, except in emergencies approved by your director. Hotel nights are covered up to 150 euros in Paris and 110 euros elsewhere in France.

Each employee receives a home-office allowance of 600 euros when they join, renewable every three years, to buy a desk, a chair or a screen. The company also reimburses 50% of public transport passes and offers a sustainable mobility package of 500 euros per year for people who cycle or carpool to work.

## 6. Equipment

New employees receive a laptop on their first day: a MacBook Pro for engineering roles and a MacBook Air or a Dell Latitude for other roles. Laptops are replaced every four years. Lost or stolen equipment must be reported to IT within 24 hours through the #it-help channel, and the theft must be declared to the police.

## 7. IT security

Multi-factor authentication is mandatory on every company account. Passwords must contain at least 14 characters and are stored in the company password manager, Keyvault; never reuse a personal password. Your laptop must lock automatically after 5 minutes of inactivity and its disk must be encrypted.

If you receive a suspicious email, do not click any link: use the "Report phishing" button in your mail client, or forward the message to security@brightwell.example. The security team runs a simulated phishing campaign every quarter, and employees who click are offered a short refresher training rather than any sanction.

Company data must never be uploaded to personal cloud storage or to AI tools that are not on the approved list maintained by the security team.

## 8. Onboarding and trial period

The trial period lasts four months for engineers and managers and two months for other roles. During the first week, every newcomer is paired with a buddy from another team who answers practical questions. A check-in meeting with the manager takes place at the end of the first, second and third month.

## 9. Performance reviews and training

Performance reviews happen twice a year, in January and July. They cover objectives, skills and career wishes, and salary reviews take place once a year, in January only. Every employee has an individual training budget of 1,200 euros per year, which can be used for courses, conferences or certifications; unused budget is not carried over.

## 10. Leaving the company

The notice period is one month for employees in their first two years and three months afterwards, unless the employment contract says otherwise. Equipment must be returned on the last working day. An exit interview with HR is offered to everyone who leaves, and its answers are anonymised before being shared with management.
